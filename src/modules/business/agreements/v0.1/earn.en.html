BIYUN Product Rules and Risk Disclosure · v0.1 · Version date: 2026-10-30

1. Scope and your confirmation

These rules apply to the flexible and locked digital-asset Earn products currently offered by BIYUN. Read the product information and these rules before deciding to subscribe. Opening a page or viewing an estimate does not complete a subscription. Selecting the acknowledgement and submitting confirms that you understand the product rules and risks applicable to that action.

Check the asset, amount, term, annual rate, early-redemption availability and automatic-processing preferences. Information that is not displayed must not be assumed to guarantee a return or processing time. If anything is unclear, consult the Help Center and the official contact channels actually published by the platform before proceeding.

2. Subscriptions, limits and processing results

Subscriptions are subject to available spot funds, account and product status, minimum amounts, asset precision, personal limits and remaining product capacity. Limits configured as unlimited are treated accordingly. New subscriptions and redemptions can be paused separately. Maintenance, settlement windows or exception handling can also temporarily restrict actions.

A successful subscription moves principal from available spot funds into an Earn position. The same principal cannot also be spent as available spot funds. Distinguish an accepted submission, a failed action and an unconfirmed result. If the result is unknown, check the original order, position and account history before submitting again.

3. Accrual dates, rates and reward precision

Accrual starts on the day after subscription, using UTC calendar days. Flexible rewards use complete holding days: the subscription day earns no reward, and the redeemed portion earns no reward for its redemption day. Tiered flexible products apply each annual rate to its corresponding principal band. The first-tier rate does not apply to all principal. If every band has an upper bound, principal above the final bound earns no reward outside those bands.

The current daily calculation uses eligible principal and the applicable annual rate, divided by 365, and rounds down to the asset's accounting precision. A daily reward below the smallest accounting unit can be zero. An estimate is not an available credited balance. An annual rate is not a daily rate and does not guarantee an asset price or a return measured in fiat currency.

A locked position saves its annual rate and term when subscribed; that position's daily rewards use the saved annual rate. Accrual starts on the next UTC day for the selected term, and maturity processing follows the last accrual day. Later flexible settlements use the product's tier configuration applicable at processing. Completed reward records and future estimates are different.

4. Redemption and early exit

When redemption is available, flexible principal can be redeemed partly or in full, subject to product and personal daily redemption limits and other applicable controls. Successfully processed principal returns to spot. Immediate credit is not guaranteed during maintenance, exceptions or pending funds processing.

Locked early redemption is available only where the product permits it and the position has not entered maturity processing. It returns principal to spot and, in the same funds operation, takes back from spot the rewards already paid for that position. The net spot increase can therefore be less than principal. If rewards to be taken back exceed the principal being returned, sufficient additional spot funds are required. Early redemption does not retain that position's previously paid rewards.

5. Automatic subscription, renewal and maturity destination

If you actively enable flexible auto-subscription, the system can use available spot funds in that asset according to the configured execution schedule, within account eligibility, minimum subscription amounts and effective limits. Disabling it stops subsequent new automatic subscriptions; it does not redeem an existing position.

If you actively enable locked auto-renewal, eligible principal can stay in Earn at maturity and create a new position using the product's then-current annual rate and term. A new term does not preserve the earlier annual rate. A selected flexible maturity destination also depends on an available flexible product in the same asset, its minimum amount and effective limits. If renewal or the selected destination is unavailable, the current implementation falls back to redemption to spot. A platform-wide pause or processing exception can delay execution; use the actual records to confirm the outcome.

6. Reward funding and main risks

Current rewards are paid to spot from the platform's corresponding reward pool. This does not guarantee external investment returns or imply an implemented on-chain strategy, restaking, onward lending or another exchange's funding arrangements. Insufficient pool funding or a system exception can leave rewards pending or delayed. Uncredited rewards are not available spot funds.

Asset prices, liquidity, issuers, systems, networks and rule changes can cause losses. This is not a bank deposit and does not claim deposit-insurance coverage, a fixed-return guarantee or protection of principal's fiat value. Do not substitute estimated rewards for an assessment of principal value and redemption availability.

7. Records, contact and version

Check positions, subscription and redemption history, reward records and spot entries to reconcile principal and rewards. Keep order identifiers and UTC times when raising an issue through the Help Center and official contact channels actually published by the platform. Never provide anyone with a password, private key or one-time authentication code.
