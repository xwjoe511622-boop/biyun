BITWINEX Product Rules and Risk Disclosure · v0.1 · Version date: 2026-10-30

1. Scope and borrowing confirmation

These rules apply to BITWINEX's current digital-asset collateralized loans. Borrowing depends on account and product status, supported assets, available funds, minimum amounts, limits and loan-to-value controls. This page does not state that every region, user or asset is eligible or that the platform holds a particular jurisdiction's licence.

Check the loan asset and amount, locked collateral, annual rate, margin-call and liquidation thresholds, fees and available repayment methods. Some settings may need to be checked in these rules, product parameters or loan records; do not assume that the confirmation dialog displays every setting. If a result is unknown, check the original order before submitting again.

2. Collateral, reference prices and LTV

Required collateral moves from available spot funds to that order's collateral account. Unreleased collateral and collateral still being processed cannot be used again. Loan and collateral amounts are measured separately in their respective assets.

Current LTV is the reference value of outstanding principal plus unpaid interest divided by the reference value of collateral. The platform uses supported index reference prices. Assets configured for a par reference use the corresponding platform reference setting, without guaranteeing an external conversion at that price. Missing or stale prices can temporarily prevent new borrowing, risk checks or liquidation.

Initial, margin-call and liquidation LTV thresholds have different purposes. Price changes, interest, additional borrowing or removing collateral can raise LTV. A margin-call threshold does not guarantee protection against liquidation. Monitor the loan and maintain a risk buffer.

3. Hourly interest in advance, compounding and parameter changes

Interest is currently charged hourly in advance. A new borrowing records its first hour of interest when it occurs; later due hours record interest at the start of each interest period. Repaying within the first hour does not recalculate by the minute or refund already recorded first-hour interest. Very small calculations can be zero because of downward rounding.

One hour's interest is currently the interest base multiplied by APR and divided by 8760, rounded down to exactly 8 decimal places. Without compounding, the base is outstanding principal. With unpaid-interest compounding enabled, it includes outstanding principal and unpaid interest. The permitted precision of principal and collateral differs from interest's fixed 8-decimal rule. Displayed decimal places alone do not establish the debt.

The loan asset's annual rate and compounding setting can change. When catching up due but unrecorded hours, including before additional borrowing, repayment, collateral adjustment or liquidation, the current implementation uses the annual rate and compounding setting read when that catch-up runs. It does not replay each historical parameter change hour by hour. Do not assume that the rate at borrowing is fixed for the entire loan or that every past, unrecorded hour will use the settings that existed at that hour. Check loan details and interest records for amounts owed.

4. Repayment and collateral adjustment

Repayment pays due interest first and principal second. The current interface can offer repayment from spot funds or conversion of collateral, subject to order and asset support. Collateral conversion obtains reference prices again at processing and rounds the required collateral amount upward. It does not guarantee execution on an external market.

After full settlement and successful funds processing, remaining collateral is released according to that order's actual records. A partial repayment does not guarantee release of all collateral. Returning or reducing collateral remains subject to initial-LTV and other risk constraints. Confirm collateral additions, repayments and returns from completed records.

Pausing new borrowing does not cancel existing debt and is different from disabling repayments for existing loans. Maintenance, a platform-wide pause, exceptions or an action already in progress can delay handling. A pending action must not be mistaken for a repaid loan.

5. Margin calls, book-mode liquidation and shortfalls

The system can initiate liquidation when the order meets its applicable conditions. Margin-call notices are supplementary and are not guaranteed to arrive in advance. Receipt of an SMS, email or site notice is not a prerequisite for liquidation. Current risk checks skip missing or stale prices, so liquidation is not guaranteed to finish immediately when a threshold is crossed.

Current liquidation is book-based and uses reference prices. It calculates collateral needed for debt and the configured liquidation fee, rounding upward to the collateral asset's precision. Taken collateral moves to the platform's corresponding liquidation account, and the remainder returns according to the records. This process does not guarantee that collateral has been sold externally at that price.

If collateral cannot cover debt and fees, all collateral for that order can be taken. The current implementation limits the fee to the coverage available, records uncovered debt as platform-pool bad debt, and closes the order with zero remaining order debt after liquidation succeeds. This version has no mechanism that automatically takes unrelated account assets to cover that shortfall. These processing rules do not guarantee the collateral's value or protection of lending principal.

6. Records and main risks

Reconcile principal, interest, repayments, remaining debt, collateral and liquidation records separately. Reference values in a record are not a fixed-conversion promise. Digital-asset loans can incur price, rate, liquidity, system, network, issuer and rule-change risks, including loss of all collateral. Returns from the use of borrowed funds may not cover interest or liquidation losses.

Pool funding, price-source or processing exceptions can affect the time needed to complete borrowing, repayment or liquidation. This page does not guarantee funding, timely liquidation, fixed interest rates or principal's fiat value.

7. Contact and version

Consult the Help Center and official contact channels actually published by the platform, keeping the original order identifier and UTC time. Never provide anyone with a password, private key or one-time authentication code. Where no genuine contact details have been published, do not trust an alleged official email address, telephone number or payment account provided by someone else.
